# Readiness contract

Use stable issue codes and three concepts:

- blocker: the next provider request would be invalid, ambiguous, or unrecoverable;
- warning: quality or continuity risk exists, but a downstream compiler can still make an executable request;
- repair step: earliest production stage that owns the missing truth.

Character authority requires four approved outputs: `portrait`, `face_closeup`, `expressions`, and `turnaround`. A single three-view sheet is insufficient.

Blocking authority requires a spatial plan plus a multi-frame motion board. The board is direction evidence only; its annotated pixels must never enter final image/video references.

Dual-anchor video prefers both K1 and Klast. Legacy imported projects may proceed with K1 and a warning; newly created strict groups should make a missing Klast blocking.

Do not use a fixed total-duration gate for production. A 15-second test and a three-minute episode share the same shot-level contracts.
